Kiddo — an app where a child aged 4-6 learns letters and numbers in Uzbek, Russian or English with a real recorded voice and a friendly mascot.
Short lessons and games; no ads; it works offline; the camera is used only if a parent turns it on.
The parent sets homework and sees the progress in a parent area.
The core app is free. Kiddo PRO costs 24,990 so'm a month or 238,990 so'm a year.
Get it on Google Play, the App Store, or the web.
