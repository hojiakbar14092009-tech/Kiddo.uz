## What would flip a no

- A hard daily time limit I set myself, plus a progress report showing he really learned letters and numbers in Russian, so it supports the tutor instead of just being screen time.
- If the kindergarten teacher used it and set homework through it, or other mothers in our Telegram group said their kids really learned to read with PRO.
- A clear list of what PRO gives over the free app, plus a daily time limit I control, and seeing in the parent area that he really learned letters after a couple of weeks on the free version.
- If after a few weeks of the free version my child really started reading letters and counting, and the kindergarten teacher or another mum I trust said PRO is worth it.
- If other parents in our kindergarten Telegram group used it and said the free part was not enough and PRO really got the child ready for school, and it was a one-time small payment instead of monthly.
- If after a few weeks on the free version I saw my child actually reading letters and counting in Russian, and the parent area showed me real progress, I would pay for the year.
- If the kindergarten teacher told me my child is behind and recommended this app specifically, I might try a month.
- If after a month of the free version my child still asks for it on their own and I can see in the parent area that they actually learned letters.
- Other mothers in our kindergarten Telegram group saying their child actually learned the letters with it, plus a one-time yearly card I could give grandma to pay as a gift for school preparation.
- If after a few weeks on the free version the parent area showed me he really learned letters and numbers, and PRO had a clear first-grade readiness program that matches what the school will test.
- Several moms in my Telegram group or the kindergarten teacher saying their kids learned Uzbek letters with it, and that the lessons stay short.
- If the kindergarten teacher or other mothers in our Telegram group said it really got their child ready for school, and I could pay a small amount through Click or Payme without a card.
