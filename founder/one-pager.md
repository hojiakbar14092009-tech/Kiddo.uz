# Kiddo "Ready for First Grade" — one page

**Problem.** Parents of 4–6-year-olds in Uzbekistan want the child ready for first grade. The free options (YouTube, global apps) don't show whether the child learns, and the Uzbek apps found are thin one-time unlocks (`competitors.md`).

**Customer.** First: Tashkent parents a year before first grade who already pay for preparation and pay subscriptions with Payme/Click. Next: Uzbek-medium families, reached through kindergarten teachers.

**Offer and price.** 10 minutes a day of letters and numbers in Uzbek, Russian or English, with a real voice. There is a parent-set time limit and a weekly report of what the child knows. Free covers one language. PRO costs **24,990 so'm/month or 238,990 so'm/year**. There is a 14-day free trial with no card, Payme/Click payment, and money back within 14 days of the first payment.

**What money is for.** None is asked for. The founder plans to start at 0 so'm. Unavoidable small costs are store accounts, a lawyer for the terms, and possibly paid hosting once there is revenue.

**The ask.** Introductions to kindergarten teachers and parents' groups in Tashkent for the founding-family test.
