# Product lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- Real craft where nobody looks: ~1,900 automated tests, an offline-capable shell, and screens ready in 0.7–1.2 s on a slowed phone. That is the kind of care parents notice without being able to say why.
- ~16,000 recorded voice clips in three languages. A 4-year-old can't read, so the voice *is* the interface. It is the hardest part to copy and it is already built.
- Children's-store rules (no ad IDs, no third-party analytics, camera only if a parent turns it on) are built into the design, not added later. That is a trust signal a parent can see.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No one thing.** Alphabets x3, numbers, math, space, games, shop with coins, studio, shorts, collection, homework, attention helper. The pitch needs a paragraph, so the product isn't clear yet. A parent can't say what Kiddo *does* in one sentence, and a child gets lost in it.
2. **PRO is undefined.** The brief doesn't say what sits behind the paywall. If the free app already does everything, PRO has no reason to exist. If it doesn't, the first five minutes may hit a wall. Either way the money moment hasn't been designed yet.
3. **Seams between systems.** Store install, a 215 MB native download, sign-in, payment (Payme/Click/store billing, still undecided), and parent consent are all handoffs that aren't built yet. One bad handoff (a long download on mobile data, a failed payment, a consent screen the parent doesn't understand) loses the family before the child sees the mascot.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- In one sentence: what does a parent get that they cannot get today? (Whether that gap is real is a /founder-consumer and /founder-competitors question.)
- Walk me through minute 0 to 5: install, who holds the phone, the first sound the child hears, the first thing the child does.
- Which half of the features would you cut tomorrow, and which one must be brilliant?
- What exactly is in PRO, and at which moment does the parent see the price? (Whether 24,990 so'm/month works depends on conversion and acquisition cost, which are unknown: /founder-cfo.)
- Which payment path, and who sees a failed charge first?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. A one-sentence promise, and the feature set cut down to what serves it. The rest stays hidden until the core is loved.
2. A written PRO definition and a mapped first-five-minutes flow, from store page to first lesson, including the download size on mobile data.
3. Five real families watched using it (parent and child, no coaching) before store submission, with what broke written down.
4. Payment method chosen and the consent and privacy text reviewed by a lawyer before launch.
